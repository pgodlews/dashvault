First-time setup wizard

Connect over Bluetooth

Power on your Pi and wait about a minute, then connect to "Dashvault Setup".

This browser can't use Bluetooth. Use Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS or Linux, or join the Pi's "Dashvault-XXXX" Wi-Fi hotspot instead. iPhones and iPads need the hotspot.

Where to keep clips

Clips stay on the Pi's SD card, with no backup yet. After setup, add your NAS or cloud storage in the web app under Settings → Archive storage.

Create your login

For the Dashvault web app. The password also replaces the default password of the Pi's pi account.

🎉

Settings saved

Your Pi will now restart, join your Wi-Fi network and install itself. Leave it powered: this takes several minutes, and longer on a Pi Zero W. If it can't join your network, its setup hotspot comes back so you can try again.

Once it's done, open your browser and go to:

http://dashvault.local

If that doesn't work, check your router for the Pi's IP address.
